============================================================
DEFINITIONS
============================================================
Main = main-analysis.R models, adjusted = same models with covariates added to the mean part.
Both are fitted on the same rows: 829 of 920 (rows with time, date, entry axis and progress). Beetles: 41 
time = time of day (centred hours), date = recording day (fixed factor),
dance = entry axis * (progress + progress^2), progress = 0 at dance start, 1 at the end.
all = date + time + dance. Q4 is run for main and all only (slow).
Contrasts, families and Holm correction as in main-analysis.R.
A contrast changes if its Holm p crosses 0.05 between main and adjusted.
